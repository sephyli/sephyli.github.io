About

I am Co-Founder & CEO of Archon Robotics, building Large Humanoid Model toward general-purpose whole-body intelligence.

I am also a PhD student at Fudan University and Shanghai Innovation Institute, conducting research at OpenDriveLab under the supervision of Prof. Hongyang Li.

My research focuses on Physical AI, end-to-end AD systems, 3D reconstruction, and generative models. I'm passionate about developing Physical AI systems that can perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world.
